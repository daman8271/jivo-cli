## Gotchas learned
- Indian Bank CC 527 = GL `2201101`. Same "bank debits principal+charge in one line" pattern as the ICICI 2549 reco — always check for this before calling small stray debits "unmatched".
- **Ilahi party reco**: full method saved to [[ilahi-party-reco-method]] — read before doing another vendor/customer ledger reco.
- Caught two of my own bugs mid-session (see [[ilahi-party-reco-method]] for detail): sign mismatch when matching Tally's signed amounts against SAP's unsigned amounts, and a missing `amtfield='pay'` kwarg that silently zeroed out VendorPayment amounts. Both caused real numbers to look "unmatched" — **always sanity-check a few unmatched items by hand before trusting an automated match's totals.**
