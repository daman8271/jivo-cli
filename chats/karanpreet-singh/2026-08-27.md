# Gmail on `jmail`, 2026-08-27

Connected **karanpreet@jivo.in** (Google Workspace) to the existing `mail-cli`
reader, which until now only spoke to Zoho and Hostinger mailboxes.

## What was added

- `mail-cli/jmail-gmail` — launcher that reads `GMAIL_MAIL_*` from the repo-root
  `.env` and maps them to the names `jmail` expects. Strips the cosmetic spaces
  out of a Google app password, defaults to `imap.gmail.com:993`.
- Four `GMAIL_MAIL_*` lines in `.env` (gitignored, this repo is public).

Needs a Google **app password**, not the account password, and 2-Step
Verification on the account. Read-only like the rest of `jmail`: opens every
mailbox with EXAMINE, never sends, flags, moves or deletes.
