## Gotchas learned
- **A/c 2549 = GL `1104107` (Oil)** — NOT in HouseBankAccounts; sits under parent `2201100 BANK CASH CREDIT LOAN` despite the 1104xxx code. Identify Oil bank GLs by account number in the GL name.
- Service Layer **cannot filter JE lines server-side** (`JournalEntryLines/any(...)` unsupported) — pull JEs by `ReferenceDate` window with `--all`, filter lines by `AccountCode` in code.
- `ChartOfAccounts.Balance` is live-to-the-second; balances quoted on 28-Jul had moved materially by 01-Aug (normal postings). Always restate the as-on date.
- Windows box has **no LibreOffice** (`recalc.py` fails: AF_UNIX); recalc via **Excel COM** (`pywin32`, `CalculateFullRebuild`) works.
