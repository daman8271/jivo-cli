## Platform

web

## Users

JIVO Wellness operators — the owner's side (Daman), the Accounts team, e-com/ops
leadership. Situation: a daily glance, often from a phone, deciding what to
produce, purchase, push, or liquidate. Not analysts; they want the answer, not
an exploration tool. [Inferred from repo CLAUDE.md + the request; not
interview-confirmed.]

## Product Purpose

Answer one question at a glance, every day: **what is in our godowns right now,
and what is more or less than normal.** Public URL, refreshed daily from live
SAP. Success = an operator opens it, knows within seconds which items are
overstocked/dead and which are short/out, and trusts the numbers.

## Positioning

Numbers are pulled live from SAP HANA (OITW/OINM) by a deterministic script and
were adversarially verified against SAP to the rupee. "Normal" is computed per
item — its own 6-month level history and 15–60 days of cover at its 90-day
consumption rate — because SAP min/max levels are unmaintained (verified:
Mart has zero set).

## Operating Context

Three companies (Oil / Mart / Beverages), 135 godowns of which many are
non-physical (fixed-assets, in-transit, virtual, wastage). Live facts the
surface must carry honestly: ~₹100.8 Cr net stock value but only ~₹52 Cr in
physical godowns — ₹45+ Cr sits in "Fixed Assets" warehouses; DEAD stock
dominates item counts (Oil: 908 of 1,376 items had zero movement in 90 days).
Quantities are PIECES (bottles), never cartons (team correction C-0001).
Money is INR, presented in crores/lakhs with Indian grouping.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Static single-file dashboard (`site/index.html`) + `site/data.json` (~770 KB),
fetched with cache-busting. No external CDNs/fonts/requests (self-contained,
fast on office wifi and phones). Public page: no credentials, no PII, read-only
data. Dark and light themes. Mobile responsive; tables scroll in their own
containers. Data vocabulary: statuses OUT / LOW / NORMAL / HIGH / DEAD;
warehouse classes physical / fixed-assets / in-transit / virtual / wastage /
non-moving / other-media; fields per item: onhand, value, out90, cover,
hist_avg, vs_normal_pct, committed, onorder, per-warehouse breakdown.

## Brand Commitments

JIVO Wellness — edible oils (olive, canola, mustard) and beverages, India.
Brand green is core to JIVO's identity [inferred from brand materials, not
user-pinned]. Voice: plain and direct, numbers first, no hype — the operator
asked for "the list, not the rationale."

## Evidence on Hand

All data real and live: `site/data.json` generated by
`pipeline/build_data.py` from SAP HANA, verified by two adversarial agents
(21 + 55 checks, totals match SAP to the rupee). Nothing on the surface is
synthetic. As-of timestamp comes from the data file.

## Product Principles

1. The answer first: more-than-normal and less-than-normal are the headline,
   not a chart gallery.
2. Never hide an ugly truth in a rollup — fixed-assets value, dead stock, and
   negative cost pools are shown and labeled, not smoothed.
3. Every number traceable: definitions of "normal" and every status live on
   the page.
4. Glanceable on a phone in a godown or a mandi, legible in sunlight.
5. Verified beats pretty: if design and clarity conflict, clarity wins
   (Operate surface).
