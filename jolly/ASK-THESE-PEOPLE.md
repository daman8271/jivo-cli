# What to ask, and who to ask it

Grouped by person. Each one is one question, in plain words, with one line on why it
matters. Answers can be one word.

## GAUTAM — the floor  ·  +91 86073 32900

**1. Do the machines stop around 4:20 pm, or do they keep running till about 7 pm and
someone enters all the production at the end?**
*Why: the app shows work stopping at 4:20. If they actually run till 7, then our whole
speed calculation is wrong.*

**2. Clear Pack, on 5 litre — how many bottles an hour, really?**
*The sheet says 3,000. The best it has ever done is 2,695, and normally it does about
1,000 to 1,500. Which number should we plan on?*

**3. Tin Head — how many tins an hour?**
*There is no number for it anywhere in the software. In August the best it did was 215.*

**4. If a run needs 10,000 caps and only 8,000 are in stock — do you start it anyway and
finish later, or do you wait?**
*Why: the computer currently refuses to start. If the floor starts anyway, we should let it.*

**5. Line clearance takes about 51 minutes, and 21 of those are waiting for QA to sign.
Is QA standing on the line or do you have to call them?*
*Why: that waiting cost us about 12 hours of production in August.*

**6. Who writes the case numbers into the app during the shift, and does anyone check them?**
*Why: the app says 59,723 cases for August, the run sheets say 88,139, SAP says about
105,000. Three different numbers.*

**7. Nobody has ever signed a waste approval — 517 entries, zero signatures since May.
Is that signature meant to be used, or is it just something the software came with?**

date, instead of typing it in the remarks box?*
*Right now it is free text like "HOLD BY NAVNEET VG". 41 bills in 90 days.*

**17. Can the dispatch team fill the planned truck date a day or two in advance instead of
on the same day?**
*The field is already there and empty. If we know a day ahead, we can plan the space.*

**18. Do customer returns and stock transfers come in through the same gate?**
*There are modules for both in the app and neither has ever been used, but SAP shows 444
