# SAP Admin

The only role in the roster that is a **system** rather than a business area —
and the one the pilot has to get past before it can touch anything.

## Who holds it

| Person | Title | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| [[Ziyaul]] | "Sap admin" | +91 93100 56401 · `ziaul2@jivo.in` |

The only person in the whole roster with a **@jivo.in** address.

## Why he is the gatekeeper

Every SAP login, licence and permission the coordination system would ever need
runs through this role. Practical consequences:

- A service account for the pilot is **his** approval to give.
- The three company databases (Oil / Mart / Beverages) are his to administer.
- If the SAP feeders break, he is the one who fixes them — and prior work found
  **three feeders silently dead**: factory GRPO (15 days), OMS quotation feed
  (6 days), e-com stock sync (73 days). Nothing monitors them. He may not know.

> [!note] Talk to him early
> This is the person to speak to *before* wiring anything in, not after it
> breaks. He is also the natural owner of the monitoring gap above.
