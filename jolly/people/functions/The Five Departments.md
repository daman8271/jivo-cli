# The Five Departments

The official org shape of JIVO's supply chain, stated verbatim in an Aug-2026
supply-chain brief — **and it does not match the roster
[[Gurvinderjeet Singh]] sent.** That mismatch is the most useful thing in this
note.

> [!quote] Brief §1
> "Today, five departments drive our supply chain independently — **packaging-material
> procurement, oil and raw-material procurement, finance, infrastructure (storage
> and filling-line capacity), and production**. Each is led by its own HOD, each
> keeps its own view of the numbers, and coordination between them is manual,
> slow, and reactive."

## The five, and who we think holds them

| # | Department | Named holder in the roster? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Packaging-material procurement** | ❌ **nobody** — and cartons are the worst bottleneck in the chain (~27 days) |
| 2 | **Oil and raw-material procurement** | ✅ [[Shunty Veerji]] ("oil purchaser") |
| 3 | **Finance** | ❌ **nobody** — [[Preshit Singh]] does billing, which is not the same thing |
| 4 | **Infrastructure** — storage and filling-line capacity | ~ [[Prince Bathinda]] and [[Honey Factory]] hold storage; **filling-line capacity has no owner** |
| 5 | **Production** | ✅ [[Gautam]] and [[Raju Veerji]] — two people, one title |

> [!warning] Two of five departments have no named HOD in the roster
> Packaging procurement and finance are named as departments in the brief and
> appear nowhere in the ten contacts. Either their HODs were among the **two
> deleted contact cards**, or they simply haven't been given yet.

## Data ownership — who owes what

Brief §5 assigns every input an owner. Five of eight come from the ERP
automatically; **only three are asked of humans**, and those three are the
entire human dependency of the system:

| Data | Source | Provided by |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly production plan | Planning sheet | **Planning** |
| Sales history (3 months) | ERP — automatic | System |
| Minimum stock levels (35%) | Derived from sales | System |
| Live stock on hand | ERP — automatic | System |
| Bill of Materials | ERP — automatic | System |
| **Lead times** | Reference template | **Procurement** |
| **Machine capacities** | Reference template | **Production / Infra** |
| **Material-to-machine map** | Reference template | **Production** |

Independent verification from the BOM spine check: lead times **are** derivable
from purchase history, so that ask is softer than it looks. **Machine capacity
is genuinely missing** — it exists nowhere in the books, which is why it has to
come from [[Gautam]] and [[Raju Veerji]].

## The end state the brief proposes

> [!quote] Brief §1 and §7
> "one HOD supported by a small team can steer the entire chain from monthly plan
> to dispatch" · "Confirmation of the **single owning HOD** and the supporting
> **team of up to five**."

**That HOD has not been named.** Five independent department heads are meant to
collapse into one owner plus five people — and who that owner is remains an open
management decision, not a settled fact.
