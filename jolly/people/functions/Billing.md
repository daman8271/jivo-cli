pairing is itself informative: the man who plans the month is the man who bills
it, so he sees both the promise and the invoice.

## Note on the definition JIVO uses

Turnover here is **invoices net of GST (`DocTotal − VatSum`) minus credit
notes**, by `DocDate`, excluding cancelled — and intercompany (23 group
CardCodes) is excluded. If the pilot ever quotes a billing figure back to him,
it has to use that definition or the number will not match his.
