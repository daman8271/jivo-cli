# Oil Purchase

The longest lead time in the chain, and therefore the earliest place a month
can be saved or lost.

## Who holds it

| Person | Their slice | Contact |
|---|---|---|
| [[Shunty Veerji]] | "Oil purchaser" — buys it | +91 98918 65729 |
| [[Gurpreet Singh Gopi]] | "Oil tracker" — follows it | +91 79825 71810 |

Whether "tracker" means tracking consignments in transit or tracking invoices
in OMS is **not established**. Both readings are plausible and they imply very
different jobs.

## Why it matters

Prior work measured JIVO's sourcing lead times from purchase history: **mustard
oil 12–50 days**, with cartons the worst packaging bottleneck at ~27 days
average. A shortfall spotted at production time is already too late — it had to
be caught here, weeks earlier.

Also established from the books: **JIVO buys loose oil, it does not crush** —
so this function is a buying operation, not a milling one.
