11Method, and the traps that would have broken this

What counts as an expense here
Every posting to the 5600000 INDIRECT EXPENSE tree — account codes 561xxxx through 569xxxx, GroupMask = 5. Direct expense (5100000), purchases (5400xx) and COGS (50000xx) are deliberately outside this. This is the "indirect expenses" head you asked for.
Net, never gross
SUM(Debit) − SUM(Credit). Several heads are provisioned monthly and reversed the next month; totalling debits alone overstated freight by ₹1.71 Cr in a previous audit.
Cost centre
SAP Dimension 3, JDT1.OcrCode3. Dimension 4 is department, Dimension 5 is state, Dimension 1 is product/vehicle. A posting with no Dimension 3 stamp lands in "(no cost centre)".
Excluded on your instruction
OcrCode3 IN ('NPD1','NPD2','OTE'). NPD 3 exists as a code in Oil and Mart but carries no postings in these four months, so it changes nothing either way.
Rounding
The HANA driver returns exact rationals, not decimals, so every sum is wrapped in TO_BIGINT(ROUND(…,0)). Per-row rounding makes a grand total differ from a directly-summed total by a rupee or two.
Trap — interest is inside
Group 561 Finance Expense is part of indirect expense. Oil carries ₹35–50 L a month of it. Excluding interest, Oil's four months are ₹1.90 / ₹2.03 / ₹4.04 / ₹0.98 Cr — ₹8.95 Cr, averaging ₹2.24 Cr a month.
Trap — Sales Realise is provision noise
In Oil, this one cost centre booked ₹1.79 Cr in June and reversed ₹1.24 Cr in July. That single head is why June looks high and July looks low. June and July taken together are clean; either month alone is not.
Trap — Mart swings the other way
Mart's ECOM and Sales cost centres both carry negative months (a reversal larger than that month's spend). Mart's June is ₹36.9 L and its July is ₹4.19 Cr for the same reason. Read Mart on a four-month average, not monthly.
Trap — one month is not a month
August 2026 is deliberately absent. Only ₹10.95 L had been posted in Oil when this was pulled, against a ₹2.6 Cr monthly run-rate. A part-posted month read as a real one would look like a 95% cost saving.
Trap — three companies, one group
Intercompany recharges (Oil ↔ Mart ↔ Beverages) appear as real expense in the paying company's books. They net out only at group level, so the group column is the honest one.

The query behind every number

SELECT IFNULL(j."OcrCode3",'(unallocated)') AS CC,
       a."AcctCode", a."AcctName",
       SUBSTRING(a."AcctCode",1,3)          AS GRP,
       LEFT(j."RefDate",7)                  AS MTH,
       TO_BIGINT(ROUND(SUM(IFNULL(j."Debit",0) - IFNULL(j."Credit",0)),0)) AS NET,
       COUNT(*) AS TXNS
FROM   <COMPANY>.JDT1 j
JOIN   <COMPANY>.OACT a ON a."AcctCode" = j."Account"
WHERE  SUBSTRING(a."AcctCode",1,2) = '56'
  AND  a."GroupMask" = 5
  AND  j."RefDate" >= '2026-04-01' AND j."RefDate" < '2026-08-01'
GROUP BY IFNULL(j."OcrCode3",'(unallocated)'), a."AcctCode", a."AcctName",
         SUBSTRING(a."AcctCode",1,3), LEFT(j."RefDate",7);
