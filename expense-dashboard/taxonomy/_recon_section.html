10Reconciliation — the team file vs SAP

A colleague's workbook, budget 3 month.xlsx, covering May, June and July 2026. Despite the filename it is not a budget: its "Budget" and "Sub Budget" columns are the names SAP gives Dimension 3 and Dimension 4, so it is actuals by cost centre. It carries only Oil's cost centres — no ECOM, SUPPLY-C or HR/ADMIN (so not Mart), no JIVOS (so not Beverages) — and it is three months against the four on the rest of this page. Everything below compares it to a live SAP read at the same grain: Oil · Dimension 3 × Dimension 4 × month · indirect expense 56xxxxx · net of credits · posting date.

The three differences that are explained

NPD 2 ↔ One Time Expense — ₹24.10 L, June
A June journal moved ₹24,10,107 out of One Time Expense into NPD 2. SAP carries both sides; the workbook has neither. Net effect on the grand total: ₹14,339. The cheapest of the three to settle — ask for June to be re-pulled.
Sales Realise — the totals agree, the months do not
Workbook ₹54,48,575, SAP ₹54,58,081 — ₹9,506 apart over three months. But the workbook puts all of it in May while SAP has ₹0 in May, +₹1.79 Cr in June and −₹1.24 Cr in July. This is the Jivo Mart advertisement provision, posted and reversed repeatedly on 30 June and 1 July: journals 224430 and 224442 are duplicates of each other, 224444 and 224665 reverse them, 224431 reverses again and 224701 reverses the reversal. Six entries, one amount.

Factory — ₹1.39 Cr, unexplained

Three questions that would close this in one round

1. Which company database, and on what date was the workbook pulled?   2. Which account range — the whole 5600000 INDIRECT EXPENSE tree, or a hand-picked list?   3. Which report generated it — SAP's Cost Accounting report, a Crystal layout, or a query export? Start with Delivery Bhakharpur: it holds nothing but 56x accounts, so whatever explains its ₹45 L almost certainly explains Factory's ₹1.39 Cr too.

Which number to use in the meantime

SAP's. Not because the ledger is infallible, but because it is reproducible: the query is on this page, it runs against the live database, and SAP's own cost-accounting report returns the same figures since no distribution rule exists to move money between cost centres. The workbook could not be reconstructed from that ledger under six different readings. Until questions 1–3 are answered, treat its Factory and Delivery Bhakharpur lines as unverified rather than wrong — and note that 34 of the 45 cells agree to within ₹13,671 net, so this is a dispute about two cells, not about the report.
