Why there's no budget-vs-actual here

SAP holds no budget for this year

I checked both places one can live, in all three companies. SAP's budget module has 4 account-lines for the whole of FY 26-27 in Oil, nothing at all in Mart, and nothing past last year in Beverages. JIVO's own budget add-on has a single ₹10,000 test row.

The last sane budget on record is Oil, April–June 2025, about ₹1.91 Cr a month. After August 2025 the entries go junk — one month has Delivery Bhakharpur budgeted at ₹5,555 crore. So the monthly actual above is the only reliable thing to track.

Which company

Three separate sets of books. They add up at group level, but never compare a line in one against a line in another.

One month on its own will lie to you

Interest is sitting in here

Bank interest is part of running cost in these books. If you only want operating spend, take the Accounts department's interest out — it is roughly four-fifths of that line.

August 2026 is not a month yet

Barely anything is posted for it, so it is kept out of every preset. You can still select it by hand from the dropdown — just don't read it as a real month.

The mistake was mine, and it is worth recording

I first reconciled this file against Oil alone and reported a ₹1.95 Cr gap with two cells I called unexplained — Factory ₹1.39 Cr and Delivery Bhakharpur ₹45 L. I even listed “Oil and Beverages added together” as a hypothesis I had ruled out. That was wrong twice over: the file does include Beverages, and my test of that idea compared at cost-centre level with mismatched blank handling, which buried the signal.

What settled it was master data. Two sub-budget codes in their file — DIG MKT and PPR MED — do not exist in Oil's chart of dimensions at all. They exist only in Beverages. And they tie to the rupee: PPR MED June, file ₹600, Beverages ₹600. DIG MKT July, file ₹20,512, Beverages ₹20,512. Those rows could not have come from anywhere else.

On the right basis the two “unexplained” cells mostly close on their own: Delivery Bhakharpur from ₹45.07 L out to ₹5.07 L, and Factory from ₹1.39 Cr out to ₹31.80 L. The lesson: establish which companies a figures file covers before calling any variance unexplained.

How I worked it out

The short version
What counts as spend
Everything posted to the 5600000 INDIRECT EXPENSE tree — codes 561xxxx to 569xxxx. Not purchases, not cost of goods.
Net, not gross
Debits minus credits. Plenty of heads get provisioned one month and reversed the next; counting debits alone overstated freight by ₹1.71 Cr once before.
Department
Not an SAP field. It comes from Accounts' own workbook, which labels every Budget / Sub-Budget pair with a department. Their labels are taken as given; the same rule is extended to combinations their file never saw, and anything I had to guess carries a flag on the row.
Cost centre and team
SAP Dimension 3 (what a bill calls “Budget”) and Dimension 4 (“Sub Budget”).
Dates
Posting date. Bills are dated the month they hit the books, not the month the vendor wrote them.
How far back
September 2024, which is where these books start. Every month since is selectable.
SELECT j."OcrCode3", j."OcrCode4", a."AcctCode", a."AcctName", LEFT(j."RefDate",7),
       TO_BIGINT(ROUND(SUM(IFNULL(j."Debit",0) - IFNULL(j."Credit",0)),0))
FROM   <COMPANY>.JDT1 j JOIN <COMPANY>.OACT a ON a."AcctCode" = j."Account"
WHERE  SUBSTRING(a."AcctCode",1,2) = '56' AND a."GroupMask" = 5
  AND  j."RefDate" >= '2024-09-01' AND j."RefDate" < '2026-09-01'
GROUP BY 1,2,3,4,5;   -- run once per company: Oil, Mart, Beverages
