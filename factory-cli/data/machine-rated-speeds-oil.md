## Derived: 1-litre capacity (rated, no downtime)

All six lines that can run 1L: 18 + 33 + 80 + 30 + 40 + 90 = **291 BPM combined**.
At rated speed that is ~17,460/hour, ~1.40 lakh per 8-h shift, ~4.19 lakh per 24-h day —
real output will be lower (changeovers, OEE). Relevant to the 1L filling capacity
crunch analysis (15.15 lakh bottles/month needed vs ~7.5 lakh practical limit).
