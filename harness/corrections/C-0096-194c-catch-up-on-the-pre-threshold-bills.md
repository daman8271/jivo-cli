# 194C catch-up on the pre-threshold bills is booked by a JV, and the entry still proceeds

## Wrong
Treated a contractor's below-threshold bills as permanently exempt once he crossed Rs 1,00,000 - deducted 1% on the crossing bill and everything after, and left the earlier bills at zero with nothing owed. NAHIM: Rs 1,135 taken on the 01-Jul bill, the 13-Jun Rs 12,500 never picked up.

## Right
The whole FY value bears 194C once the Rs 1,00,000 aggregate is crossed. The crossing bill carries its own 1% on the A/P invoice; the TDS on the EARLIER below-threshold bills is a shortfall JIVO books through a separate Journal Voucher. The A/P entry must PROCEED - never hold the draft over it - but the catch-up amount is always flagged to Accounts so the JV is made.

## Evidence
Daman's ruling 2026-09-18 on the live figures already in C-0092 (Divjot 2026-09-12, FY26-27, DocDate ge 2026-04-01, Cancelled tNO): NAHIM PAN DPYPA3165J, Oil VENDA001714 - bill 1 13-Jun Rs 12,500 TDS 0, bill 2 01-Jul Rs 1,13,500 TDS Rs 1,135, i.e. 1% of the second bill alone. 1% of the Rs 1,26,000 aggregate is Rs 1,260, so Rs 125 is the catch-up that belongs in a JV.

## Rule
<!-- ONE line, imperative. This is the only part injected into every session.
     Keep it under ~200 chars. Write it so it is actionable without context. -->
194C: when a contractor crosses Rs 1L FY, deduct 1% on that bill, PROCEED with the draft, and FLAG the TDS owed on his earlier below-threshold bills - that catch-up is booked by a separate JV.
