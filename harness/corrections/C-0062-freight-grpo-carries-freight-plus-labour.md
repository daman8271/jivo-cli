# Freight GRPO carries freight PLUS labour — the bilty's whole charge

## Wrong
The GRPO line total is the transporter's FREIGHT figure; labour/loading is a separate charge that does not belong on the goods receipt.

## Right
Where a transporter bills LABOUR (or loading/extra) in its own column, it is part of that bilty's charge and goes on the GRPO with the freight. The GRPO total for a bilty = freight + labour, so the GRPOs for a bill add up to its G. TOTAL (gross). The 1% 194C transporter TDS is taken later, on the A/P invoice, not here.

## Evidence
Confirmed by Daman 2026-08-31 on MAHAVIR bill 678 (freight 73,922 + labour 1,898 = G.TOTAL 75,820). Corroborated: every past MAHAVIR Oil A/P invoice is exactly 99% of the GRPO total it copies (4,000->3,960; 22,500->22,275; 63,894->63,255; 35,138->34,787), i.e. the GRPO carries the gross and 1% TDS is applied at the invoice. Oil drafts 55600-55613 built this way total 72,440 = 75,820 less the Beverages bilty 3670 (3,380).
