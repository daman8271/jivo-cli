# Electricity is one shared plant bill — Beverages carries the larger share because water is the heavy user

## Wrong
Treated the Rs 26,04,139 Kundli electricity bill as JIVO Oil's cost and loaded all of it into Oil's conversion cost, then concluded from Oil's books alone that 'the books hide the electricity, it never touches an expense line'. Both wrong: it overstated Oil's factory cost by Rs 14.3 lakh a month and mislabelled a scope error as an accounting defect.

## Right
Daman, 2026-08-30: 'In the factory for the beverages, when we are producing water, water takes up a lot of electricity since the machine is heavy and very strong. It sucks a Rs 26 lakh bill for electricity and water was the main player in that.' The Kundli plant runs Oil AND Beverages off one supply, and WATER PRODUCTION is the dominant consumer. The split is already in the books, per company, and Beverages carries the bigger share. Charge each company only its own booked power; never put the whole plant bill on one of them, and never diagnose a gap from a single company's ledger.

## Evidence
Live 2026-08-30, May-Jul 2026 monthly average from JDT1 x OACT. JIVO_BEVERAGES_HANADB 5680011 ELECTRICITY = Rs 15,76,107/mth (plus 2180005 PROVISION FOR ELECTRICITY -563,896). JIVO_OIL_HANADB 5680011 ELECTRICITY Rs 8,92,587 + 5100020 ELECTRICITY DIRECT EXPENSE Rs 2,81,923 = Rs 11,74,510/mth. Sum Rs 27,50,617 ~= the Rs 26,04,139 plant bill. Beverages = 57% of it. Bev production ran 2.0-3.7 M pcs/month over the same window (OWOR). Stated by Daman 2026-08-30.
