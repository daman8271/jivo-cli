# Never split a cash voucher across expense heads unasked

## Wrong
Booked voucher 453 (Rs 2,083) wholly to 5650015 FUEL - VEHICLES as the sheet said, although Rs 1,100 of it was a reversing camera fitted to the XL6; then split it only after being told.

## Right
A cash voucher may carry bills belonging to more than one expense head, but the operator decides whether to split it. Ask before splitting; do not split on your own reading of the bills.

## Evidence
Daman, 2026-09-19, asked whether to split by head automatically: 'Do not split it yourself ask from me first.' Voucher 453 = CNG Rs 983 + camera Rs 1,100 (RD 1 Stop Drive Tech, 01-09-26), split into draft 57460 lines 1 and 8 only on his word.

## Rule
<!-- ONE line, imperative. This is the only part injected into every session.
     Keep it under ~200 chars. Write it so it is actionable without context. -->
Cash voucher covering more than one expense head: do NOT split it yourself - show the operator what the bills behind it are and ask. Splitting unasked is a correction waiting to happen.
