Red flag Nº 3

The “returns explosion” is largely invoice recycling

fake sales, fake returns

Oil’s return rate genuinely tripled in two years (1.7% → 4.3%) — but the documents show the pattern: invoice → full credit note days later → identical re-invoice. R K Worldinfocom: billed ₹3.33 L + ₹15.87 L on 12-Nov; credited both to the rupee on 13-Nov; re-billed 29-Nov; credited 13-Dec; re-billed 21-Jan — one consignment documented five times (three “sales”, two “returns”, no goods moving). Kailian Foods (47%) and Comed Chemicals (33%) show the same signature. Consequences: sales and returns both overstated, GST documentation exposure, and no distributor-behaviour problem where one was claimed. Separately real: 62% of Oil returns (₹6.04 Cr) carry no salesperson code — returns cannot be netted against any incentive scheme today.

Red flag Nº 4

The books do not reconcile themselves — on either side

₹292.57 Cr gross

SAP shows ₹292.57 Cr of “open” purchase invoices in Oil against a true payable of ₹105.94 Cr — payments are almost never applied to invoices (the down-payment tables are empty; everything sits on-account). AL GHURAIR shows ₹93.70 Cr “unpaid” while its actual balance is a ₹2.35 L debit in JIVO’s favour. Round 3 proved the debtor side rots the same way: B S A Engineering’s ₹1.11 Cr “overdue” had been received two weeks earlier and left on-account — the collections team was about to chase money already in the bank. No cash is missing — but every payment run and every aged-debtor report built on document status is wrong. Fix: monthly internal reconciliation per partner, both ledgers; never work from the open-document lists.

Red flag Nº 5

GSTIN hygiene — credit taken, counterparties unverifiable

₹1.41 Cr+

Found while killing the ITC finding: JIVO Oil has claimed ₹1.41 Cr of GST input credit from suppliers with no GSTIN recorded in the ERP — every rupee at risk in a 2A/2B mismatch — plus a ₹52.1 L dead ITC asset parked in the GL. The pattern repeats across the file: HS Filling (₹2.90 Cr paid out, ₹2.40 Cr still outstanding, no GSTIN), AB Enterprises (₹14.04 Cr of lifetime loose-canola sales to a customer with no GSTIN on file, near-zero recorded cost, credit limit back-fitted to the debt plus ₹3). Rule worth making today: no vendor payment and no customer credit above ₹10 L without a GSTIN in the master.

Documents & masters

  • Open A/P credit notes >90 days on the monthly close checklist
  • Signed stock-count reference on every COGS-contra goods receipt

Credit & payroll

  • Credit limits set by policy, never back-fitted to the outstanding balance
  • July payroll gated on 100% TankhaPay attendance approval — live gap the day of the pull
  • Per-head imprest caps keyed on the JWPL code across all three companies

Purchasing & systems

  • PO release blocked when months-of-cover exceeds policy; close stale POs for MRP hygiene

Method, sources & certifications

How the file was built

Phase 0 — reconnaissance of live data paths. Phase 1 — eight finder agents in parallel, one lens each (vendors · receivables · duplicate payments · purchase price · stock · returns · payroll · expenses); 146 hypotheses, every claim backed by SQL actually executed. Phases 2–3 — 25 adversarial skeptic runs across three rounds (28–29 Jul), each round’s briefs carrying the traps that killed the previous round’s claims; one run severed by a SAP outage was resumed to completion, and agents that stalled mid-return were recovered from the notes they had already written. Phase 4 — this document.

Sources — all read-only

Direct SAP HANA SQL (port 30015) over JIVO_OIL_HANADB · JIVO_MART_HANADB · JIVO_BEVERAGES_HANADB — the actual books, with server-side aggregation; the Service Layer as fallback; TankhaPay payroll workbooks (mispunch + 30-day attendance). Not one write was made to any system. Calibration anchor: Oil FY25-26 turnover ₹193.91 Cr.

Caveats

Figures are as-of-29-Jul ledger reads; “annual” figures are last-twelve-month run rates; workout haircuts (AB 50%, oil QC 75%) are judgment calls stated in the notes; nothing here is a statutory audit.
