# Statutory interest & penalties — real, but less than half the headline

**Sweep claim (rank 55):** ₹18,92,063/yr of statutory interest and penalties, "100% avoidable".
**My verdict: REVISED → ₹7.83 lakh/yr bankable** (gross statutory cost booked FY25-26: ₹16.64 lakh).

## CFO summary in plain language

JIVO really is paying the taxman for being late. In FY25-26 the three companies booked
**₹16.64 lakh** of genuine statutory interest and penalties — income-tax interest, TDS
interest, GST late fees, one EPF penalty. That part of the sweep is real and I could
re-derive it from the journals line by line.

Two corrections bring the number down:

1. **₹2.48 lakh in the original claim was not a late-payment penalty at all.** ₹1.28 L was
   an income-tax *refund* written off against an old assessment demand; ₹1.00 L was two
   round ₹50,000 "penalty charges" reimbursed to an employee's imprest account with no
   payee and no statute named; the rest was an electricity-board late fee, a transporter
   penalty and a few traffic challans. None of those is fixed by a statutory calendar.

2. **"100% avoidable" is not true of the biggest slice.** ₹12.81 lakh of the ₹16.64 lakh is
   interest on income tax under s.234B/234C — the price of paying advance tax late. To pay
   it on time JIVO has to draw the Indian Bank CC at **8.25% p.a.** instead of running an
   interest-free arrear at 12% p.a. The *net* saving is 3.75%, not 12% — so only about
   **₹4.00 lakh** of that ₹12.81 lakh is real money. The rest of the pool (TDS interest,
   GST late fees, EPF penalty) is flat dead-weight with no float benefit and *is* 100%
   avoidable.

Net bankable: **₹7.83 lakh a year**, at zero cost, from a payment calendar with a named owner.

**Encouraging signal:** in FY26-27 to date (Apr–Jul 2026) the statutory penalty accounts in
all three companies are **empty** — the only movement is a ₹23,830 *credit* in Oil reversing
an electricity charge out of PENALTY CHARGES. Somebody may already have tightened this. The
calendar should lock the improvement in rather than assume it.

The pool more than doubled year on year, so "recurring" is fair. But note the honest caveat:
income-tax and TDS interest are booked as **periodic manual journal entries** (May, Sep, Mar),
so four silent months do not prove the problem is gone — though the **May-2026 slot where
FY25-26's ₹2.22 L TDS interest landed is empty**, which is a genuine positive.

**Sensitivity:** the ₹1,39,821 GST penalty and ₹9,846 GST interest were paid on the Delhi
GSTIN in Jan–Feb 2026, the same window in which Oil booked ₹71.5 L of ITC reversals narrated
*"for audit period from 2020-21 to 2023-24"* (GL 5660008). If those cash penalties belong to
that historical GST audit rather than to routine current-period late filing, they are a
**one-off, not recurring** — and the recurring bankable figure drops from ₹7.83 L to
**₹6.33 L/yr**. Accounts should confirm from the DRC-03 / challan copies.

## Key SQL

Fiscal-year movement in every statutory account, per company (this is the shape that killed
the claim — the sweep totalled account balances; I totalled journal lines and then read every
narration):

```sql
SELECT j."Account", a."AcctName",
       CASE WHEN j."RefDate" >= '2026-04-01' THEN 'FY26-27'
            WHEN j."RefDate" >= '2025-04-01' THEN 'FY25-26' ELSE 'PRE' END AS "FY",
       COUNT(*) AS "N", CAST(SUM(j."Debit"-j."Credit") AS DOUBLE) AS "NET"
FROM JIVO_OIL_HANADB.JDT1 j
JOIN JIVO_OIL_HANADB.OACT a ON a."AcctCode" = j."Account"
WHERE j."Account" IN ('5660009','5660011','5660012','5680005','5680034')
GROUP BY j."Account", a."AcctName",
         CASE WHEN j."RefDate" >= '2026-04-01' THEN 'FY26-27'
              WHEN j."RefDate" >= '2025-04-01' THEN 'FY25-26' ELSE 'PRE' END;
```

Line-level narration + the offsetting GL, which is what separated real penalties from
refund write-offs and imprest reimbursements:

```sql
SELECT j."TransId", j."RefDate", j."Account", a."AcctName",
       CAST(j."Debit" AS DOUBLE) AS "DR", CAST(j."Credit" AS DOUBLE) AS "CR",
       j."LineMemo", o."TransType"
FROM JIVO_OIL_HANADB.JDT1 j
JOIN JIVO_OIL_HANADB.OJDT o ON o."TransId" = j."TransId"
LEFT JOIN JIVO_OIL_HANADB.OACT a ON a."AcctCode" = j."Account"
WHERE j."TransId" IN (SELECT "TransId" FROM JIVO_OIL_HANADB.JDT1
                      WHERE "Account" IN ('5660009','5660011','5660012','5680005')
                        AND "RefDate" >= '2025-04-01' AND "RefDate" < '2026-04-01')
ORDER BY j."RefDate", j."TransId", j."Line_ID";
```

Independent cross-check by account *name* rather than account code, excluding genuine
borrowing cost (5610001/5610002/5610004 bank & unsecured-loan interest, 1106102/1106122 FDR
accrued interest, 1213005, 5500006) — returned the same seven buckets and nothing else.

## Action

**Owner: Head of Accounts / Compliance (with the Group CFO as escalation).** Zero cost.

1. **Statutory payment calendar in writing, one named owner per due date** — monthly TDS
   (7th), quarterly TDS returns (31 Jul / 31 Oct / 31 Jan / 31 May), monthly GSTR-1 & 3B
   (11th / 20th), annual GSTR-9 & 9C (31 Dec), advance tax (15 Jun / 15 Sep / 15 Dec /
   15 Mar), EPF & ESIC (15th). Calendar reminders 5 days and 1 day ahead, all three companies.
2. **Quarterly advance-tax computation with the auditor** before each instalment date —
   this is the ₹4.00 L slice and it is a *forecasting* fix, not a discipline fix. Pay the
   instalment even if the CC has to fund it; 12% penal interest beats 8.25% CC every time.
3. **Open the two ₹50,000 imprest "penalty charge" attachments** (SAP AtcEntry 63720 and
   128018, A/P DocNums 725053123 and 726013139, vendor ORGV000136). Identify the authority
   and the offence. Round ₹50,000 twice, paid straight to an employee's bank account, is a
   control finding in its own right regardless of what the penalty was for.
4. **Confirm the Jan–Feb 2026 GST challans** (payments 126467130–133, 226466564–565) against
   the DRC-03s — routine late fee or the 2020-21→2023-24 audit settlement? That decides
   whether ₹1.50 L of this is recurring.
5. **Monthly one-line MIS**: balance of 5660009 / 5660011 / 5660012 / 5680005 across all
   three companies. Target zero. It has been zero for four months — keep it there.

Offset worth knowing: Oil also *collected* ₹1,23,920 of "PENALTIES & FINES COLLECTED"
(GL 4200004) from its own counterparties in FY25-26, so the group's net penalty position is
better than the expense line alone suggests.
