Bills ↔ payments mapping

which payment settled which bill, and what was held back

Each book on its own

Three separate SAP companies, ledgers and GST registrations. The report above combines Oil + Beverages only — and every combined figure shows its Oil/Bev split. Mart is its own report and is never in any combined number. A vendor code in one book is a different account in another.

How to read this

  1. TDS comes from the invoice, not the payment. The TDS shown is OPCH.WTSum on the bill. At payment, SAP's VPM2.WtAppld is 0.00 across the board. “Paid gross — TDS not deducted” means the full bill amount was paid and SAP's WTApplied on that invoice is 0: a TDS-compliance question, never an overpayment.
  2. “Other deduction” is an unexplained residual. It is GROSS − TDS − DISCOUNT − NET PAID, a balancing figure, not a verified deduction — a shortage/damage claim, or a bill settled by journal. When no payment touches a bill it is shown as not computed, never as zero.
  3. DocStatus is unreliable at JIVO (C-0019). A bill settled by a manual journal entry stays “O” in SAP. Nothing here is aged or called “open” from DocStatus; status comes from the money — allocation lines and PaidToDate.
  4. On-account payments are money paid against no bill. They have no allocation line at all. They sit in their own bucket — “Paid, not yet matched to a bill” — and in the on-account figure above. They are the reason the same transporter keeps getting searched.
  5. Two reports: Oil + Beverages together, Mart separate. Combined Oil+Bev figures always carry their split (“Oil ₹X + Bev ₹Y”) because they are two SAP books — codes and documents live per book, so the drill-down shows one book at a time and the roster labels every row. Mart is never mixed into any figure.

Window: bills and payments dated from 1 Apr 2025 (FY25) so that every FY26 payment can find the bill it settled; the default view is FY26. A bill a payment points at but dated before 1 Apr 2025 renders as “(invoice outside window)” with its DocEntry. Paid can exceed gross in a year because FY26 payments also settle FY25 bills — that is real. Every payment line type (invoice, credit note, journal, on-account, contra) is counted; none is dropped. Figures are pulled from live SAP B1 (HANA) by pipeline/build.py at the stamped time and published read-only; nothing on this page writes to SAP.
