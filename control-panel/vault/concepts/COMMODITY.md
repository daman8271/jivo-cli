# COMMODITY vs PREMIUM

Within the [[OILS]] segment, SKUs are graded **COMMODITY** (bulk, low-margin staples) vs **PREMIUM** (branded, higher-[[REALISE|realisation]]). The split drives separate order-in-hand rows and margin analysis (note the API key `u_main_group` on the commodity variant vs `main_group` on the standard one).
