# Sales Channels

JIVO splits its go-to-market into four channels, targeted and reported separately across the Sales dashboard:

- [[GT]] — General Trade (distributor/retail)
- [[MT]] — Modern Trade (organised retail)
- [[ROI]] — Rest of India (geography rollup)
- [[ECOM]] — E-Commerce (marketplaces / q-commerce)

Each channel carries its own [[TGT]] / [[DONE]] / [[BAL]] and [[REALISE]], drilled to customer and [[Main Group]].
