# Warehouses

**Definition.** JIVO's **plant / depot / warehouse master** — the physical stock locations where finished goods and raw material sit. Stock and non-moving reports break quantity down per warehouse (`wh → {warehouse: qty}`), and production feasibility checks raw-material availability by warehouse.
