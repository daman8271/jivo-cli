# Wellness–Mart Reconciliation

**Definition.** Inter-company reconciliation between the two JIVO ledgers/entities — **JIVO Wellness** and **JIVO Mart** — matching stock movements and ledger balances so inter-company transfers agree on both sides (mismatches = leakage to investigate).
