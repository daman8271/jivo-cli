# Segments: OILS vs BEVERAGES

JIVO's business is reported in two **segments**, each on its own data track (separate targets, docs and receivables):

- [[OILS]] — edible oils (core; split into [[COMMODITY]] vs premium)
- [[BEVERAGES]] — juices / wellness drinks

Most Sales/Accounts screens carry a segment toggle; the beverages track has dedicated endpoints ([[beverages-data]], [[beverages-docs]], [[customer-aging-beverages]]).

**Used by:** [[sales-dashboard]], [[customer-aging]], [[non-moving-stock]] · APIs [[segment-targets]], [[sales-data]], [[beverages-data]].
