# Open questions — what no agent can answer

Six questions that need a person. Every one of them is cheap to answer and expensive to
guess at, and between them they gate most of [[Ten-Moves]].

---

## 1 · What do Akal Dairy, Akal Bakery and Akal Modi Khana actually supply?

**Highest-value unanswered question in the exercise.** Ask: the Trust.

Eternal University's ₹2.51 Cr of audited food spend is the largest figure in this vault. If
it flows to the campus's own in-house units — which are ARY's *creditors*, on 251 vouchers
since June 2026 — then the "₹4–5 Cr unserved institutional wallet" is a mirage and
[[Ten-Moves]] move 1 changes shape entirely.

Also worth asking in the same conversation: is EU's ₹1.96 Cr of mess meal charges paid to
those units or to outside vendors?

→ [[Institutional]]

## 2 · Is the ₹47/L milk price deliberate subsidy or neglect?

**₹66,951/yr.** Ask: ARY's manager or the Trust.

Purchase cost went ₹45 → ₹55 over the year; the retail price stayed at exactly ₹47.00 across
all 16,105 litres with zero variance. Subsidised milk for boarding children at a charitable
trust's shop is entirely plausible — [[Pricing-Fairness]] shows ARY profiteers nowhere else,
which makes subsidy the more likely reading.

Either way it should be **recorded** as a subsidy. Today it is absorbed silently and shows
as a healthy +35% margin in every report.

→ [[Below-Cost-Leak]]

## 3 · Does Akal Charitable Hospital hold a drug licence, and does it run a dispensary?

**₹0 to ask.** Ask: the hospital, inside the same Trust.

Two consequences either way: it may be able to host or sponsor a licence, and whatever it
dispenses free to residents is demand a retail pharmacy will never see. 100 beds, 11 doctors,
~120 OPD patients a day.

→ [[Pharmacy]]

## 4 · Can FusionERP8 store a batch number and expiry date?

**The real gate on the pharmacy.** Ask: the FusionERP8 vendor.

`ProductChildMaster` has **111,021 rows and zero populated `ExpDate` or `MfgDate`.** A
pharmacy cannot legally operate without batch and expiry tracking — recall, Schedule H1
records, expiry returns. This is a software question, not a retail one, and it must be
answered before a licence is worth applying for.

→ [[Pharmacy]] · [[Data-Quality-Traps]]

## 5 · Does stitching labour sit inside the unstitched-suit margin?

**₹11 lakh rides on the answer.** Ask: the ARY accountant.

₹17.06 lakh paid to 18 tailors against ₹5.92 lakh billed for stitching. Either the labour is
absorbed into garment cost — plausible, ARY sells unstitched suits — or the service is
under-recovered. One is normal accounting; the other is an ₹11 lakh leak.

→ [[Services]]

## 6 · What is Akal Academy Baru Sahib's actual enrolment?

**±350 students, and it moves every per-resident figure in this vault.** Ask: the Principal.

Derived as ~1,250 from published CBSE board registrations (Grade X 98, Grade XII 57) across
15 grade levels. The only published figure — "over 1,550" — traces to ~2017 boilerplate from
when board cohorts were 40–50% larger. True figure sits somewhere in 1,100–1,450.

A second ±487 hangs on whether AIRWE's rural women are counted inside Eternal University's
1,102 or are additive.

→ [[Population]]

## One cheap test, not a question

**Order the two existing monthly-pack diaper codes once. ~₹5,000.**

[[Baby-Care]] has two readings that produce an identical signal — a small infant base, or
unstocked bulk packs — and no amount of analysis separates them. Stocking the packs for one
cycle and watching whether they move settles it for the price of a rounding error.
