# Ten moves, ranked by return on effort

Ordered so each step is cheap relative to what it unlocks, and so nothing expensive happens
before the question it depends on is answered.

**Read [[Fleet-Method]] before totalling anything here.** These are not additive, and the
research fleet's own aggregate (₹13.3 Cr against a ₹7.55 Cr business) is what happens when
you try.

---

## 0 · Stop selling 27 SKUs below cost

**₹3.06 L/yr leaking now · ₹0 cost · hours · blocker: none**

The only thing in this vault losing money today. **"Am Lower" alone is 1,512 units at
−₹90.89 = −₹1.37 lakh**, fixable with one price change. Loose milk is −₹66,951 — cost went
₹45 → ₹55 while the price stayed hard-coded at ₹47.00 across all 16,105 litres.

One judgement call belongs to ARY: subsidised milk for boarding children may be deliberate.
If so it should be **recorded** as a subsidy, not absorbed as a phantom +35% margin.

→ [[Below-Cost-Leak]] · [[Data-Quality-Traps]] · `ary assort leak`

## 1 · Ask the Trust what its own food units supply — then propose two-way trade

**₹29–36 L gross profit/yr if winnable · ~nil investment · one conversation**

₹2.51 Cr of audited food spend on the same campus, 1.1% captured. **But do not open with a
quote** — Akal Dairy, Akal Bakery, Akal Modi Khana and Akal Catering Mess are ARY's own
*creditors*. Pitch consolidated procurement and two-way trade, not displacement.

Do **move 0 first**: the institutional bulk twins are all sold below cost, so winning more
volume at negative margin makes the business worse.

## 2 · Ask the hospital what drug licence it holds

## 6 · Build a premium and gifting tier in the institution-specific range

**Undisruptable category · weeks · blocker: none**

one price. Kirpan's ₹250–399 lines sold on one bill each. Kara and Kangha have one SKU apiece.

thinnest-priced shelf in the shop.

## 7 · Add narrow, high-turn retail ranges — one at a time

**₹71 L gross profit at stake · months · blocker: capital, licences**

The Fruits & Veg playbook is how: **~55 SKUs, full run-rate in month two, grow frequency not
range.** The benchmark names the four, in order: **dairy** (92 selling lines vs 864),
**staples and masala** (451 vs 1,913), **fruits & veg** (132 vs 271, on the best-turning
counter), **baby care** (no product group at all). Fund each by deleting from beauty,
household and kitchen, which carry ~5,400 lines more than benchmark.

## 9 · Fix the counting before adding high-value small items

**4.1% of revenue at risk · weeks · blocker: ERP recipe posting**

Post canteen recipe consumption, make transfers two-sided, stop negative stock at the till,
delist the 13,284 non-selling lines. **Move the annual count from August to the January
trough** — counting at 35–49% of normal footfall costs a fraction of the disruption.

Adding cosmetics, pharma and mobile accessories to a shop already losing 4.1% would compound
the problem, not reveal it.
