## Headline

**The control-panel CLI is a window onto a Django analytics app, and that Django app is itself
a window onto SAP.** Nearly every *fact* it serves is a SAP B1 row (verified live against HANA,
field-for-field, on five independent entities). What is genuinely new here is **not data, it is
calculation** — plus a thin but real layer of **native planning/annotation data** (targets, claims,
aging remarks, rate-list scenarios, credit locks, RBAC) that lives only in the Django DB and has
**no SAP table anywhere**.

Bucket split across 25 business entities: **17 M / 0 F / 7 N / 0 X / 1 U**.

## 3. LIVE VERIFICATION — five independent field-for-field matches against HANA

These are the strongest evidence in this report. Left column = value the Django app returned
(captured in `recon/`, 2026-07-23); right column = value I pulled from SAP HANA just now.

**(a) Dispatch details → `OINV` user fields.** Control panel `dispatch-details` row:
`{inv_no: 626070454, code: CUSTA001073, dispatch: 2026-07-22, biltydate: 2026-07-22, bilty: "3698",
transporter: "Mahaveer Transport", vehicle: "HR67C1036", mobile: "6006399745"}`

```sql
SELECT "DocNum","CardCode","DocDate","U_Dipatch_Date","U_BiltyDate","U_BilltyNumber",
       "U_TransporterName","U_VehicleNoM","U_Mob_No","U_ARNO","U_OMS_Order_No"
FROM "JIVO_OIL_HANADB"."OINV" WHERE "DocNum"=626070454;
-- 626070454 | CUSTA001073 | 2026-07-22 | 2026-07-22 | 2026-07-22 | 3698 |
--   Mahaveer Transport | HR67C1036 | 6006399745 | NULL | NULL
```
**Exact match on all 8 fields.** Freight/bilty/driver data *looks* like native app data (it is not
in stock SAP B1) but JIVO added it as SAP **user fields** on `OINV`/`ORDR`. This is the single most
important finding for the "joined vs sourced-from" trap.

**(b) Customer master → `OCRD`.** App row `{code: CUSTA001085, name: "A K ENTERPRISES",
main_group: "HORECA", credit_limit: 15.0, balance: 0.0, city: "DELHI"}`
```sql

FROM "JIVO_OIL_HANADB"."OCRD" WHERE "CardCode" IN ('CUSTA001085','CUSTA000165','CUSTA000872');
```

Exact. (GSTIN comes from `CRD7`, not `OCRD.LicTradNum` which is NULL — a join, still SAP.)

**(c) Daily production → `OWOR`.** App row `{doc: 726202786, item_code: PM0000121,
warehouse: BH-BS, planned: 9084, completed: 9084, user: "SHAHRUKH"}`

I could not explain this and am flagging it rather than hiding it: either the vault sample was

## 4. What is genuinely NEW — and where it lives

I searched SAP for anything holding the app's planning/annotation data. It is not there.

**SAP user-defined tables in `JIVO_OIL_HANADB` (`SELECT TABLE_NAME FROM SYS.TABLES WHERE
SCHEMA_NAME='JIVO_OIL_HANADB' AND TABLE_NAME LIKE '@%'`)** — ~90 tables, all belonging to SAP
add-ons or master-data lookups: `@BRAND @CHAIN @MAIN_GROUP @ITEM_SKU @ITEM_SUBGRP @ITEM_VARIETY
@ITEM_UNIT @BUDGET/@BUDGET1 @QC_I/@QC_O @SERVER @OZIA/@OZIAR @ZIA_DL_LIMIT @ZIA_DL_OLIMIT
@UNE_* @UTL_* (+ `@A…` archive twins)`. I inspected the plausible candidates:
- `@BUDGET`/`@BUDGET1` — columns `U_BUDGET, U_SUB_BUDGET, U_MONTH, U_FIXED_AMOUNT, U_V_AMOUNT`.
  A **cost-budget** dimension, not litre/realise sales targets. Not the control panel's targets.
- `@ZIA_DL_LIMIT` — `U_UserCode, U_Limit, U_OldSAPOpen`: a per-SAP-user document limit for an
  add-on. Not the credit-limit page.
- `@OZIAR` — `U_DocType, U_User, U_Start_Date, U_Hours, U_Active`: SAP authorisation windows.

**Column search across all three company schemas:**
```sql
SELECT SCHEMA_NAME,TABLE_NAME,COLUMN_NAME FROM SYS.TABLE_COLUMNS
WHERE SCHEMA_NAME LIKE 'JIVO%' AND (UPPER(COLUMN_NAME) LIKE '%TARGET%'
      OR UPPER(COLUMN_NAME) LIKE '%TGT%' OR UPPER(COLUMN_NAME) LIKE '%CLAIM%');
```
→ every hit is a stock SAP B1 internal column (`TargetType`/`TargetEntr` = document-flow pointers,
`ClaimRefun` in tax tables, `AWL5.TgtFld` in workflow). **No sales-target table, no claims table.**

**Postgres cluster:** `postsql dbs` → 15 DBs (`test_supabase, factory_flow, jivo_ecom,
order_management, oms_backup, OMS_TEST, jivo_site, Ecommerce, fs, test_order_management,
test_test_supabase, CRM, postgres, task, po_db`). `postsql search claim | aging_remark | rate_list |
realise` → **0 rows each**. The control panel's own store is **not** in this cluster.

**Conclusion:** the native data (targets, claims, aging remarks, closing remarks, rate-list
scenarios, credit locks, users) lives in the Django app's own database on the `103.89.45.75` box,
which I could **not** reach or identify (engine unknown — Django default would be SQLite or a local
Postgres/MySQL). That is a stated coverage gap. What I *can* assert with high confidence is the
negative: **it is not in SAP, and not in the Postgres cluster I can see.**

### Is anything pushed back INTO SAP? (the F question)
**No evidence of any feeder path.** Every mutating endpoint the vault documents
(`save-targets`, `save-closing-remark`, `claims/save|delete|upload`, `rate-list/save|delete`,
`aging-remark*`, `credit-lock|unlock`, `realise-calculator/upload|order-upload`, `/api/users/save|delete`)
is described as writing the app's own store. The strongest statement is in
`vault/api/aging-remark.md:12` — *"These are a Control-Panel-local overlay — SAP data is never
modified"* — and `:26`, quoting the app's own confirm dialog: *"cannot be undone. SAP data is
untouched."* Combined with the absence of any SAP UDT that could receive them, I rule **0 F entities**.
Caveat: this is `code`+`doc` evidence about write paths I deliberately did **not** execute and whose
server-side implementation I cannot read. Confidence 85, not 95.

## 5. Freshness / staleness

- The app exposes `POST /realise/api/sales-data/ {refresh: bool}` ("force server recompute /
  cache-bust", `cli/jivo-spec.yaml`) and `GET /realise/api/sales-pulse/`, a change-fingerprint the
  dashboard polls every ~30 s to detect that "the underlying SAP data moved". **Both imply a
  server-side cache between SAP and the API.** I could not measure its TTL or lag — the app is
  unreachable and I have no server code. Treat control-panel numbers as *near-live but cached*.
- The CLI adds a **second** staleness layer of its own: `jivo sync` writes API responses into a
  local SQLite store and `--data-source local|auto` will serve from it (`internal/cli/data_source.go`).
  A `jivo` answer can therefore be two caches away from SAP.

## 6. New data vs new calculation — the distinction that matters here

Things this app computes that SAP does **not** hold as a stored value, but which are derivable
entirely from SAP rows (so: **new calculation, not new data** — bucket M):
- REALISE ₹/L (net value ÷ litres, with `OITM.U_IsLitre`/pack-size conversion)
- Aging buckets 0-30/31-60/61-90/91-120/121+ and the B2B/B2C split
- Required Limit = (ledger + OIH) × 1.02
- DaysInStock / DaysSinceMoved / "non-moving" cut, last-buyer attribution
- Production feasibility (max producible FG from component stock via BOM)
- MATCHED / MISMATCH / INCOMPLETE reconciliation status + ₹1.0 tolerance
- OIH premium/commodity split, DRR, historical trailing-average realise, sales-pulse fingerprint

Things that are **new data** (bucket N) — a human typed them into this app and SAP never sees them:
targets (all five flavours), the claims register incl. hold/approval workflow, per-invoice aging
remarks + special-price overlays, per-party delivery/closing remarks, saved rate-list/realise
scenarios, the credit-limit lock, and the app's users/permission groups.

### Caveat on the sales aggregates (honest negative result)
I tried to re-derive the app's headline `sales-data` numbers from SAP and **could not reconcile them**:
```sql
SELECT I."U_TYPE",I."U_Sub_Group",SUM(L."LineTotal")
FROM "JIVO_OIL_HANADB"."INV1" L JOIN "JIVO_OIL_HANADB"."OINV" H ON H."DocEntry"=L."DocEntry"
JOIN "JIVO_OIL_HANADB"."OITM" I ON I."ItemCode"=L."ItemCode"
WHERE H."DocDate" BETWEEN '2026-07-01' AND '2026-07-22' AND H."CANCELED"='N' AND I."U_TYPE"='PREMIUM'
GROUP BY I."U_TYPE",I."U_Sub_Group";
-- OLIVE 76,185,527 | CANOLA 29,210,746 | GROUNDNUT 26,587,237
```
vs the app's captured JUL-2026 figures: OLIVE 53,017,309 · CANOLA 9,569,487 · GROUNDNUT 26,193,223.
GROUNDNUT is within 1.5 %; OLIVE and CANOLA are not. A per-customer breakdown shows JIVO MART
(`CUSTA000606`, intercompany) alone is ₹53.6 M of the OLIVE figure — so an intercompany and/or
hidden-sales exclusion is clearly applied, but excluding Mart overshoots in the other direction
(₹22.6 M). **The likeliest explanation is that I do not know the exact date range of the 2026-07-23
recon capture**, which was not recorded. I am reporting this as unreconciled rather than inventing
a rule. It does not change the bucket: the *inputs* are provably SAP rows; the *aggregation rules*
are the app's and are not reproducible from outside without the Django source.

## 7. Coverage gaps (stated plainly)

1. **No live app calls.** The control panel is office-network-only and timed out from here. All
   app-side payloads are the 2026-07-23 recon captures. Rulings are therefore `code`/`schema`/`doc`
   on the app side and `live` on the SAP side.
2. **Django source never read.** I inferred Django→SAP from response shapes + field names + the
   `schema=jivo_oil` toggle, then *confirmed by matching values in HANA*. I never saw the ORM/SQL.
   So "Django reads HANA directly" is inference (confidence ~90); "the data originates in SAP"
   is proven (confidence ~99).
3. **The Django DB itself was never located.** Not in the Postgres cluster; engine/host unknown.
4. **`/api/cogs/` is OTP-gated** — `recon/apiprobe_api_cogs_.json` = `{"error":"Permission denied"}`.
   Bucket U. COGS/margin could be SAP-derived (item cost) or a native cost model; I did not check.
5. **`/inventory/oih-vs-stock/` 404s server-side** — feature not built. Nothing to map.
6. **Write endpoints deliberately not executed** (Rule 0), so the F ruling rests on doc+code.
7. **Beverages track** assumed to mirror the oil track against `JIVO_BEVERAGES_HANADB`; I confirmed
   that schema exists but did not value-match a beverages row.
